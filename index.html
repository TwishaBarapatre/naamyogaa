About Us

Authentic Yoga,
Modern Approach

Looking for authentic yoga rooted in tradition?

With NaamYoga, you don't need to travel to an ashram or attend crowded studios. You can now access genuine, traditional Indian yoga from the comfort of your home.

Through our online yoga classes, we bring you centuries-old practices adapted for modern lifestyles. Our sessions are led by experienced Indian yoga instructors who don't just teach poses—they guide you through the deeper philosophy of yoga, focusing on breath, posture, awareness, and healing.

Whether you're a beginner, senior, pregnant mother, or someone dealing with specific health concerns like knee pain, sinus, or stress, our classes are tailored just for you.
